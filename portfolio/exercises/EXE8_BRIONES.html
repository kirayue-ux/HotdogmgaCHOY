🌸 Cherry Blossom

Cherry blossom is a beautiful flower known for its soft pink and white petals. It represents beauty, hope, and new beginnings.

In Japan, cherry blossoms are called Sakura. Their short blooming season reminds people to appreciate beautiful moments because nothing lasts forever.

“Under Sakura skies so bright, 🌸
Soft petals dance in morning light.
Each little bloom, a dream anew,
A gentle reminder to bloom too.” 🌸
