Cherry Blossoms

The Beauty of Sakura

A Story Written in Pink

Soft petals dance upon the breeze,
Whispering gently through the trees,
Pink blossoms shine beneath the sun,
Reminding us that spring has begun.
They bloom so softly, then they fall,
Like little dreams that touch us all,
Their beauty fades but leaves a glow,
A precious memory we can know.
So let the cherry blossoms be,
A symbol of hope and harmony,
For though their season may be brief,
They bring the heart a quiet peace.
"Their beauty fades but leaves a glow."
